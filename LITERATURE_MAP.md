## Appraisal to do when writing the thesis

For every cited paper, record design, sample, task, assistant, measured construct, result, and limit. Check publication version and DOI. Keep model-calibration studies in a separate background paragraph from human confidence studies. The decisive gap statement is narrow: **the assigned orientation versus observed mode, exact proposal confidence, verification timing, and final security retention together in a two-class agent-assisted study**. Do not claim that security review or poisoned AI studies have never been done.
