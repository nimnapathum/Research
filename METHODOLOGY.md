The current method is [NATURAL_WORKFLOW_METHOD.md](NATURAL_WORKFLOW_METHOD.md), protocol natural-2026-10-13-r2. The previous controlled-checkpoint method is preserved in [the archive](archive/2026-10-13-controlled/METHODOLOGY.md). It is not the participant protocol for the natural ZIP. This entry point avoids two conflicting current methodologies.
