# Natural-task forms and interview

Protocol natural-2026-10-13-r3. Questions are research adaptations, not a validated psychometric scale. Use approved consent/information text separately; portal credentials do not establish consent.

Before coding, collect role, paid-development years, JavaScript/SQL/file-API familiarity, security training, agent-use frequency, Antigravity experience and usual review frequency. The natural portal filters direct SQL/path recognition questions from the old background form to reduce immediate teaching/priming. Self-report familiarity does not establish security skill; use neutral practical screening and acknowledge residual skill differences. If objective security knowledge is tested after both tasks, label it as post-task knowledge rather than unaffected baseline.

At each task's final snapshot, `study/rate.mjs` records two 0–100 probabilities for the exact feature requirements, reasons, willingness to submit, agent-shaped parts and stated reliance. It saves task ID, version, final hash and timestamp. Participants may run their own tests; ratings precede researcher oracle feedback/debrief. These are security beliefs about code, not a standalone validated trust scale.

Upload manifest/rating first and complete the portal after-task form promptly. It asks known-next-step frequency, approach/knowledge seeking, task familiarity, difficulty, time pressure, overall agent reliance and a concrete mode example. These support the manipulation check and interpretation, but observed mode comes from independently coded intent. Upload the larger files afterward. Task 2 still requires Task 1 submission and form completion.

After both tasks, collect carryover, change in review approach, willingness to rely without another security check and notes for interview. Then follow [the natural codebook's replay questions](study-system/instruments/NATURAL_CODEBOOK.md). Record replay before debrief and link interview recording to both tasks. Current task uploads do not include a separate interview receipt; researcher must record its approved storage location/hash manually.

Replay uses predetermined events including no-check cases and neutral prompts about goals, understanding, continuation and checks. Do not show a vulnerability or tell the expected mode before the answer. Preserve participant explanation and contemporaneous evidence separately. Prior replay/evaluation precedent and limits are in [the audit](RESEARCH_AUDIT.md). Older proposal-rating forms are [archived](archive/2026-10-13-controlled/INTERVIEW_AND_FORMS.md).

## Question and measure rationale

| Current item(s) | Why collect them | Basis and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Role, paid years, JavaScript/SQL/file familiarity, security training, agent frequency and Antigravity experience | Describe eligibility and possible skill or tool-familiarity differences between the two task periods. | [Barke et al.](https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~npolikarpova/publications/oopsla23-copilot.pdf) recorded language/tool experience; [Kaur et al.](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity22/presentation/kaur) motivates transparent participant sampling. These exact scales are ours; role and paid years are imperfect skill proxies. |
| Usual review frequency | Describe prior reliance habits that may help explain observed checking. | Developer evaluation is directly studied by [Khalid et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21020). Self-report is not proof of what happened in either task. |
| Two final-feature security probabilities and reasons | Compare stated security belief with independently assessed security of the exact submitted snapshot. | [Perry et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.03622) motivates belief/outcome pairing; [Brier](https://doi.org/10.1175/1520-0493%281950%29078%3C0001%3AVOFEIT%3E2.0.CO%3B2) motivates probability scoring. Wording and two-item schedule are ours; a Brier score is not pure calibration or a trust scale. |
| Known next step, option seeking and a concrete example | Check whether the assigned work structures actually elicited different interaction purposes. | Adapted from [Barke et al.'s](https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~npolikarpova/publications/oopsla23-copilot.pdf) mode descriptions. These answers supplement independent video/transcript coding; they do not assign mode on their own. |
| Task familiarity, difficulty and time pressure | Detect the main rival explanation introduced by the unfamiliar interface. | These are design-validity checks chosen for this study, not a validated workload instrument. Compare completion and active time alongside them. |
| Agent reliance, willingness to submit and after-both willingness to rely without another security check | Interpret security confidence alongside reliance decisions. | [Lee and See](https://doi.org/10.1518/hfes.46.1.50_30392) motivates appropriate reliance; Perry and Khalid motivate studying developer belief and selection. Our items are contextual reports, not a validated trust trait. |
| Carryover and change in review approach | Identify learning or strategy transfer between the two tasks. | This is an original within-participant validity check; interview explanations cannot by themselves remove carryover bias. |
| Replay questions about goal, prior approach, understanding, continuation and checks | Explain ambiguous episodes and whether observed application represented human inspection or endorsement. | Barke's observation and [Mozannar et al.'s](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.14306) retrospective coding support contextual replay. Our wording and event-selection rule need a pilot comprehension and coder-agreement check. |

The executable portal wording is [forms.json](study-system/instruments/forms.json), the final-code rating wording is [natural_rate.mjs](study-system/pilot/natural_rate.mjs), and the replay prompts are in [NATURAL_CODEBOOK.md](study-system/instruments/NATURAL_CODEBOOK.md). Freeze these versions together with the task stimulus before the main sample.
